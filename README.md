# Home Teacher - Your Smart Personal Tutor
$SELECTION_PLACEHOLDER$![cover](https://github.com/me-wsantos/home-teacher/blob/main/assets/capa.png?raw=true)

Welcome to **Home Teacher**, the innovative app designed to revolutionize the way you study! Imagine having a personal assistant available 24/7, ready to create personalized study plans, recommend the most relevant YouTube videos for your learning, and even answer your questions in real-time through an intelligent chatbot. Home Teacher leverages the power of Microsoft Azure intelligent agents to provide a dynamic, efficient, and fully tailored learning experience. Get ready to optimize your studies and achieve your academic goals with Home Teacher by your side!

### **Core Principles:**
- **Inclusion**: We developed Home Teacher to meet the diverse learning needs of students, considering different learning styles, paces, and potential challenges. We aim to provide accessible and adaptable resources to ensure all students can benefit from the platform.
- **Personalization**: Home Teacher offers individualized study plans and content recommendations tailored to each student's interests, knowledge level, and learning goals. We believe a personalized approach maximizes each student's potential.
- **Empowerment**: Our goal is to empower students to take control of their learning journey. By providing clear study plans, relevant resources, and support for answering questions, Home Teacher aims to increase students' autonomy and confidence in their studies.
- **Transparency**: Study plan and video recommendations, as well as chatbot responses, are designed to be clear and understandable. We strive to explain, in an accessible way, how the system arrives at certain suggestions, allowing students to trust the information provided.
- **Fairness and Impartiality**: Our algorithms are designed to avoid biases that could lead to unfair or skewed recommendations. We work to ensure all students receive high-quality study suggestions and resources, regardless of their academic background, origin, or other personal characteristics.
- **Privacy and Security**: Protecting students' data is fundamental. We implement strict security and privacy measures to ensure their personal information is handled ethically and responsibly, used only to optimize the learning experience within Home Teacher.
- **Responsibility**: We take responsibility for the impact of Home Teacher on students' learning experiences. We continuously monitor system performance and user feedback to ensure alignment with our principles and promote effective and positive learning.

## 💡 Features and How Home Teacher Optimizes Your Studies

Home Teacher offers a set of intelligent tools to transform your learning experience:

### **1. Intelligent Study Plan Creation:**

* You specify the subject you want to study, and Home Teacher, using Microsoft Azure intelligent agents, creates a personalized study plan.
* The system suggests a logical sequence of topics, estimates the time needed for each, and organizes the content optimally for your learning.
* **Benefit:** Say goodbye to indecision about what to study next! Have a clear and efficient roadmap to cover all necessary content.

### **2. Intelligent YouTube Video Recommendations:**

* For each topic in your study plan, Home Teacher searches for and recommends the most relevant and educational YouTube videos.
* Our intelligent algorithms analyze video content, ratings, and other metadata to ensure you access the best visual learning resources available.
* **Benefit:** Quickly find high-quality visual explanations that complement your studies and make complex concepts easier to understand.

### **3. Interactive Chatbot for Questions:**

* Have a virtual assistant available 24/7 to answer your questions about the subject you're studying.
* Powered by Microsoft Azure intelligent agents, our chatbot can understand your doubts and provide clear and concise explanations, helping you overcome learning blocks.
* **Benefit:** Don't get stuck on questions! Get instant answers and keep progressing at your own pace.

With Home Teacher, you have a smart and comprehensive study partner ready to help you achieve your academic goals more effectively and efficiently!

The solution consists of the following main components:

- **Azure AI Agent Service**: A fully managed platform that simplifies and accelerates the creation of secure, scalable, and high-quality AI agents with significantly less code.
- **Azure Static Web App**: A service that automatically builds and deploys full-stack web applications on Azure from a GitHub repository.
- **Next.js**: A React framework that provides additional structure and features for web application development, such as routing, server-side rendering, and performance optimizations.
- **React**: A JavaScript library for building interactive and reusable user interfaces (UI) based on components.
- **TypeScript**: A superset of JavaScript that adds optional static typing, enabling error detection during development and improving code maintainability.
- **Tailwind CSS**: A utility-first CSS framework that offers pre-defined classes for quick and consistent styling of HTML elements.

### **Prerequisites**
- Azure account.
- Node.js and Next.js installed.

##### 1 - Clone the repository
  `git clone https://github.com/me-wsantos/home-teacher.git`

##### 2 - Install dependencies
  `npm install`

##### 3 - Configure environment variables

##### 4 - Start the application
  `npm run dev`
